Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

Hasaniya Financial Services, LLC ("HFS," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, hasfinancial.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact form or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • The content of any message or files you submit to us
  • Any information you voluntarily provide by phone, email, or in person when scheduling a consultation

We do not use cookies or analytics tracking on this website beyond what is required to operate standard site functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Provide bookkeeping, tax, and business support services you request
  • Communicate with you about your account or engagement with us

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form submission service. Information you submit through the contact form is transmitted to Formspree in order to deliver your message to us. Please refer to Formspree's own privacy policy for details on how they handle data in transit.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Client Financial Information

If you engage HFS for bookkeeping, tax preparation, or other financial services, any financial or personal information you provide as part of that engagement is handled in accordance with applicable professional and legal confidentiality standards, and is used solely to provide the services you have requested.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request that we delete information that is not otherwise required to be retained by law or professional obligation.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@hasfinancial.com or (909) 677-3278.
